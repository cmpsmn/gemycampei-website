Choosing where to say your vows is the most exciting part of planning an elopement in the Dolomites, and the hardest, because there are simply too many beautiful places. These are the places I have photographed and love, with honest notes on how to reach them, when the light is best and how busy they get. If you are just starting to plan, begin with my guide on [how to elope in the Dolomites](/journal/how-to-elope-in-the-dolomites/).

## Lago di Braies (Pragser Wildsee)

The emerald lake at the end of the Prags valley in South Tyrol is the most romantic place in the Dolomites: wooden rowing boats, a boathouse jetty, a little stone chapel on the shore and the steep walls of the Seekofel above the water.

- **Best light:** sunrise, when the lake is still and often misty.
- **Crowds:** very busy from mid-morning, especially in summer.
- **Access:** a short walk from the car park. In high summer, road access during the day is limited and needs a reservation, which makes an early start even more worthwhile.
- **Good to know:** autumn, with golden larches and fresh snow on the peaks, is my favourite season here.

See a [moody dawn elopement](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/lago-di-braies-elopement-s-and-j/) and an [autumn session](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/lago-di-braies-couple-session-c-and-jp/) at the lake.

## Alpe di Siusi (Seiser Alm)

Europe's largest high-alpine meadow: rolling green hills, wooden huts and the Sassolungo and Sciliar on the horizon. It is the gentlest place in the Dolomites, perfect for proposals and couples who want big views without steep paths.

- **Best light:** early morning and the last hour before sunset.
- **Crowds:** spread out over a huge area, so it rarely feels busy.
- **Access:** the road up is closed to most private cars during the day; the cable car from Siusi takes you up. From there it is about a 45-minute walk to the classic view. If you'd rather not walk, a taxi service can be arranged.

See a [surprise proposal on the Alpe di Siusi](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/alpe-di-siusi-proposal-c-and-z/).

## Val di Funes (Villnöss)

A green farming valley closed off by the jagged Odle (Geisler) peaks, with two of the most photographed churches in the Alps: Santa Maddalena and the little chapel of St. John in Ranui, alone in a meadow.

- **Best light:** golden hour, when the Odle glow pink.
- **Access:** easy by car. The chapel of St. John stands on private land and access to its meadow is regulated, so we plan the timing ahead. There is a small entrance fee of about €5 per person.

See a [post-wedding session in Val di Funes](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/val-di-funes-couple-session-a-and-h/).

## Passo Gardena (Grödner Joch)

The high pass between Val Gardena and Alta Badia, right below the Sella group and the Cir peaks. A few steps from the road you stand in meadows with rock towers all around.

- **Best light:** evening and sunset, when the towers turn orange.
- **Access:** by car or bus, with short, gentle paths.
- **Crowds:** not as crowded as many other places in the Dolomites. The road is busy by day, and the meadows are calm in the hour before sunset.

See a [golden hour session at Passo Gardena](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/passo-gardena-couple-session-k-and-b/).

## Passo delle Erbe (Würzjoch)

My favourite quiet pass: wide meadows and larch forests facing the mighty Sass de Putia, with old wooden huts dotted around. Even in summer it stays calm.

- **Best light:** late afternoon and sunset.
- **Access:** by car, with paths starting at the road.
- **Perfect for:** proposals, intimate vows and honeymoon sessions.

See a [secret proposal](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/passo-delle-erbe-proposal-t-and-k/) and a [honeymoon session](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/passo-delle-erbe-couple-session-e-and-n/) here.

## Cadini di Misurina

A cluster of needle-sharp towers near the Tre Cime di Lavaredo, and one of the most dramatic views in the Alps. The famous viewpoint is reached by a short but steep walk from the Tre Cime toll road, with a narrow last section.

- **Best light:** sunrise or sunset; at midday the path is full of hikers.
- **Access:** a helicopter flight is one of the popular ways to get up. If we hike, plan a package of at least 4 hours.
- **Parking:** a parking reservation of about €40 per car is required and must be booked in advance.
- **Good to know:** sturdy shoes are essential, and the dress travels in a bag.

See a [sunrise session at Cadini di Misurina](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/cadini-di-misurina-couple-session-k-and-e/).

## La Val (Wengen) in Val Badia

A small Ladin village with open pastures, old barns and the pale wall of the Sas dla Crusc behind it. Short paths lead to meadows and forest edges where you can be completely alone.

- **Access:** a short hike of about 20 minutes up. If you'd rather not hike and want a little adventure, you can rent e-bikes to ride up.
- **Best light:** around sunset.
- **Good to know:** up there is a small hut where you can grab drinks and food.
- **Perfect for:** an intimate elopement far from the tour buses.

See an [elopement in La Val](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/la-val-elopement-r-and-d/) and a [summer couple session](/dolomites-elopement-photographer/la-val-couple-session-a-and-k/).

## More places worth considering

- **Seceda** above Val Gardena, with its famous sharp ridge, reached by cable car from Ortisei. The cable car costs about €70 per person, and entering the viewpoint trail about €5.
- **The Tre Cime di Lavaredo**, the three famous peaks, reached by the same toll road as the Cadini viewpoint. If you don't want to hike up to the Cadini, the Tre Cime are an epic backdrop for a shorter session, about 2 hours instead of 4.
- **Lago di Anterselva, Lago di Dobbiaco and Lago di Misurina**: calm alpine lakes and a great choice if you want to avoid the crowds of Lago di Braies.
- **Passo Giau** near Cortina d'Ampezzo, a high pass with wide views, easy to reach by car.
- **A place of your own**: a mountain hut terrace, a hidden lake or a meadow only locals know. Some of my favourite moments happened in places without a name on the map.

## How to choose

Think about how you want the day to feel, not only how it should look. Do you want a lake, peaks or open meadows? How far do you want to walk? Which season?
