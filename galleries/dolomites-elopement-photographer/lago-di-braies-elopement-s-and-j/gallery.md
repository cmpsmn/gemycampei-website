## Eloping at Lago di Braies

**Lago di Braies** (Pragser Wildsee) is one of the most photographed lakes in the Alps, and still one of the most magical places to elope in the **Dolomites**, if you come at dawn. Before sunrise the lake is quiet, mist often hangs over the water and the wooden boats wait at the boathouse. By mid-morning in summer it fills with visitors, and road access during the day is limited and needs a reservation, so an early start makes everything easier. Rain is no reason to cancel: grey light turns the emerald water even deeper. [How to plan your elopement](/journal/how-to-elope-in-the-dolomites/).
