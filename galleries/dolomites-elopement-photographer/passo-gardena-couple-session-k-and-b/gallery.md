## Golden hour at Passo Gardena

On summer evenings the sun sets behind Val Gardena and lights the rock towers above **Passo Gardena** (Grödner Joch) in warm orange. Trails lead from the pass through high meadows towards the Cir peaks and the Sella group, gentle enough for a lace gown and a long veil. Because the pass lies on a busy road, the hour before sunset is when it empties and turns calm. For a **couple session** in the **Dolomites** that looks like a high-alpine adventure without a long hike, it is hard to beat.
