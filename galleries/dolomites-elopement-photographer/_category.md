    answer: "My favourite windows are **June to mid-July**, with wildflowers and thawed alpine lakes, and **late September to mid-October**, with golden larches and crisp light. August is the busiest month, and from November to April many lifts and huts close, unless you want a snow adventure. Sunrise gives the softest light and the fewest people. [Read the season guide](/journal/best-time-to-elope-in-the-dolomites/)."

I was raised between the quiet majesty of the **Italian Dolomites** and the vibrancy of Bangkok, and these mountains still feel like home. Here I photograph **intimate elopements, civil weddings, surprise proposals and couple sessions**, in the mountains I know by heart and wherever else your story leads, worldwide and especially across **Europe**.

From sunrise vows at an alpine lake to a quiet exchange of promises on a mountain pass, I help you choose the place, the season and the light, in **South Tyrol**, **Trentino** and the **Belluno Dolomites**. I know which spots are quiet at dawn, which are easy to reach in a wedding dress and where to go when the clouds roll in.

From pre-wedding gatherings and private mountain vows to honeymoons, I document intimate connections with quiet elegance, using timeless digital frames, subtle 35mm film grain and nostalgic Super 8 motion, capturing honest romance wherever your story takes you.
