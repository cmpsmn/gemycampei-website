Journeying from Bangkok to the dramatic peaks of **South Tyrol** to make a dream in the Alps come true. Surrounded by sweeping alpine meadows and towering, jagged limestone cliffs at **Passo delle Erbe**, *T & K* shared a private **proposal** against an epic mountain backdrop.

Captured in complete secrecy before turning into a quiet, unscripted celebration, their story balances the grand scale of the **Dolomites** with raw, honest emotion.

## A proposal at Passo delle Erbe

For a **surprise proposal**, a place needs two things: a breathtaking view and a way to stay unseen until the right moment. **Passo delle Erbe** (Würzjoch) in South Tyrol offers both. The meadows below the Sass de Putia are open and quiet, with rocks and larches that make it easy for a photographer to wait out of sight. The pass is reachable by car, so there is no long walk with a ring in your pocket and nerves in your stomach. Late afternoon and sunset give the warmest light.
