Tracing the steep ridges of **Cadini di Misurina** at first light. Traveling from New York to the heart of the **Dolomites**, *K & E* hiked towards the sharp limestone peaks long before dawn, the crisp alpine wind moving through a silk gown as night gave way to deep mountain blue.

Captured in those quiet, fleeting moments between blue hour and a golden **sunrise**, their **pre-wedding session** balances raw alpine scale with an intimate, unscripted atmosphere.

## About Cadini di Misurina

The **Cadini di Misurina** are a cluster of needle-sharp limestone towers just south of the Tre Cime di Lavaredo, in the Belluno part of the **Dolomites**. The famous viewpoint is reached by a short but steep walk from the Tre Cime toll road, with a narrow final section, so sturdy shoes are a must and a gown travels in a bag. Sunrise and sunset are the only times the ridge is calm; at midday the path fills with hikers. It is one of the most dramatic places in the Alps for a **pre-wedding session**.
