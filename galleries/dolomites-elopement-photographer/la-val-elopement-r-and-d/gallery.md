Tucked away in the quiet sanctuary of **La Val** in **Val Badia**, far from the alpine crowds, *R & D* set out on a short, peaceful hike to a hidden mountain haven.

Framed by a sweeping gown and towering **Dolomites** peaks, they exchanged personal vows in total solitude, surrounded by untouched nature and an unexpected, delicate swarm of alpine butterflies. An intimate **elopement** in its purest form.

## Eloping in La Val

Above **La Val**, a Ladin village in **Val Badia**, short paths lead from the last farms to meadows and forest edges where you can be completely alone. That is what makes it special for an **elopement**: vows spoken in silence, with no audience but the mountains. The walk is easy enough in a gown with comfortable shoes underneath, and the Sas dla Crusc glows in the evening light. A symbolic ceremony like this needs no town hall; [my elopement guide](/journal/how-to-elope-in-the-dolomites/) explains how to plan one.
