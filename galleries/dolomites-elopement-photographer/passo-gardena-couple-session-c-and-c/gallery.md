Journeying all the way from Texas to the rugged heights of **Passo Gardena** (Grödner Joch), *C & C* celebrated their **honeymoon** surrounded by sweeping views of the **Dolomites** peaks.

Wandering the high mountain pass at **golden hour**, their session was an effortless, unscripted celebration of their new chapter, and a timeless souvenir of their European getaway.

## About Passo Gardena

**Passo Gardena** (Grödner Joch) is the high mountain pass between Val Gardena and Alta Badia, at about 2,100 metres, right below the rock walls of the Sella group and the jagged Cir peaks. It is easy to reach by car or bus, and a few minutes' walk from the road leads to meadows with a view in every direction. That makes it perfect for **honeymoon sessions**: maximum drama with minimum effort. Golden hour lights up the rock towers, and the pass is a good starting point for the rest of your trip through the **Dolomites**.
