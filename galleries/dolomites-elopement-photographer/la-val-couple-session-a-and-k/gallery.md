Soft clouds, wide green meadows and pale rock walls above **La Val** in **Val Badia**. *A & K* spent a relaxed summer day wandering the alpine pastures, past weathered wooden huts, a wayside cross and fields full of daisies.

Between quiet, close moments and running hand in hand down the slopes, their **couple session** in the **Dolomites** was light, playful and completely unscripted.

## About La Val

**La Val** (Wengen) is a small Ladin village on a sunny terrace above **Val Badia**, with open pastures, old wooden barns and the pale wall of the Sas dla Crusc (Heiligkreuzkofel) behind it. It has none of the crowds of the famous lakes and passes, which makes it ideal for relaxed **couple sessions** where you can simply walk, talk and run through the meadows. Summer brings wildflowers and green hills; autumn turns the larches gold. [Discover more quiet places in the Dolomites](/journal/best-elopement-locations-in-the-dolomites/).
