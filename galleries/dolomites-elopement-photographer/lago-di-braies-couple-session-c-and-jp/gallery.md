## Lago di Braies in autumn

**Lago di Braies** (Pragser Wildsee) lies at the end of the Prags valley in South Tyrol, below the steep walls of the Seekofel. In summer it is one of the busiest places in the **Dolomites**, and road access during the day is limited and needs a reservation. Autumn is its secret season: from late September the larches turn gold, the crowds thin out and the first snow often dusts the peaks. The wooden rowing boats at the boathouse, the path around the lake and the little chapel on the shore all make beautiful, calm backdrops.
