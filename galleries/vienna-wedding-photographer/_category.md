Your wedding is a reflection of your story, and it deserves more than a template. From intimate civil ceremonies to multi-day destination celebrations, I document real weddings with raw emotion, refined composition and organic light. Based in **Vienna** and the **Dolomites**, I am available worldwide, especially across **Europe**.

In Vienna I photograph weddings in baroque palaces, Gothic churches and registry offices, from a quiet civil ceremony for two to a long summer celebration with family from around the world. Beyond the city, I follow couples to castles in the Austrian countryside and to **destination weddings** across Europe and further.

Every gallery combines modern digital photography with classic **35mm analog frames** and optional **Super 8 motion film**, capturing authentic connection and timeless atmosphere without staged poses.
