Surrounded by the grand architecture of **Schloss Hernstein** in Lower Austria, *Kyo* and *Benedikt* celebrated their marriage with family and friends who traveled from Korea, Afghanistan, the US and beyond.

Their **castle wedding** was a soulful blend of heritage and playfulness: a stunning ceremony, a nod to Korean culture with a **hanbok** wardrobe change, and an epic DIY karaoke party under the castle roof. Pure connection, rich culture and unscripted joy.

## About Schloss Hernstein

**Schloss Hernstein** stands in the Lower Austrian countryside, less than an hour's drive south of **Vienna**. The castle got its romantic historicist look in the 19th century, when it was rebuilt by the architect Theophil Hansen, and it is surrounded by a large park with old trees. For a **castle wedding** it combines a ceremony outdoors, a dinner in historic halls and a party under the castle roof, close enough to Vienna for guests arriving from abroad.
