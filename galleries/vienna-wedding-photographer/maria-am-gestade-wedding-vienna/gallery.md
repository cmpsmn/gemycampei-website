Bound by music in the heart of **Vienna**. Violinists *Ugnė* and *Krzysztof* gathered an intimate circle of family for a deeply personal **church wedding** at **Maria am Gestade**, one of the city's oldest Gothic churches.

From the sacred ceremony to a small orchestra performance and a stroll through the old town towards the iconic **Hotel Imperial**, their day was a grounded, music-filled harmony of pure connection.

## About Maria am Gestade

**Maria am Gestade** is one of the oldest churches in **Vienna**, a Gothic church in the first district, built high above what was once the bank of a Danube arm. Its slender tower ends in a delicate openwork dome, and inside, tall stained-glass windows fill the nave with soft, coloured light. For a **church wedding** in the old town it is intimate and full of atmosphere, and the lanes around it lead straight to the heart of the city for portraits afterwards.
