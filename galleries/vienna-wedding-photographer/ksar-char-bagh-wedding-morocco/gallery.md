Two unforgettable days in the heart of **Morocco**. Surrounded by the striking architecture of **Ksar Char Bagh** in Marrakech, *Leonie* and *Yassine* began their weekend with an intimate, moonlit welcome dinner.

The wedding day unfolded with rich tradition and high energy: a morning **Moroccan civil wedding**, a stunning ceremony under the desert sun and a poolside dinner beneath the palms. After an unforgettable **Amaria** (العمارية) entrance, the night dissolved into wild celebration until the early morning.

## About Ksar Char Bagh

**Ksar Char Bagh** is a palace hotel in the Palmeraie, the palm grove on the edge of **Marrakech**, built in the style of a fortified Moorish palace with high walls, courtyards, water basins and gardens. For a multi-day **destination wedding** it offers everything in one place: a welcome dinner, the ceremony and a long night of celebration. Moroccan weddings often include rich traditions like the **Amaria**, where the couple is carried in on a decorated throne.
