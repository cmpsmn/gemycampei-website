From long distance to a home together in **Sweden**. *Araya* and *Christoffer* gathered their favorite people in the quiet countryside near **Falun** to celebrate the start of their next chapter.

Simple, intimate and full of genuine laughter, their **wedding** was a beautiful reflection of a love that bridged worlds and finally settled in one place.

## About Falun

**Falun** lies in Dalarna, the region many Swedes call the heart of their country: red wooden houses painted with the famous Falu red from the old copper mine, birch forests, lakes and long, soft northern light. A countryside **wedding** here feels relaxed and close to nature, with summer evenings that seem to never end. For me, **destination weddings** like this are about the people as much as the place.
