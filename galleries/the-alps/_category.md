I was raised between the quiet majesty of the **Italian Dolomites** and the vibrancy of Bangkok, and these mountains still feel like home. Here I photograph **intimate elopements, civil weddings, surprise proposals and couple sessions**, from misty sunrises at Lago di Braies to golden larches in Val di Funes.

From pre-wedding gatherings and private mountain vows to honeymoons, I document intimate connections with quiet elegance, using timeless digital frames, subtle 35mm film grain and nostalgic Super 8 motion, capturing honest romance wherever your story takes you.
